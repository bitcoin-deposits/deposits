# bitcoin deposits
## oshihokololo

onkalo ya yela yokulipititha oimaliwa mefimbo limwe okupitila mointanete otai ka pitika omalipilo a tumwe thiluthilu okupuka komulipiti umwe okuya kumukwao meendelelo nomeilongo shike. lightning network otai gandja oshitukulwa shombangi, ashike omauwa a fimanenena otaa kanitha ngeenge ombelewa yomukalelipo yontatu otai pumbwa okuungaunga noukwatya wayo ponhele yoye. ohatu ulike ombangi youpyakadi ou hatu longitha ledger omapekaapeko noshinima sha collateral. operator ohava tumina omafetululo a ledger kovakalele vakwao, hava nyola omapekaapeko a dja omashiivifo. wallet ohadi tumina oumbangi wokuhena ouyelele kovakalele ovo, ovo hava kala ve shi oukwatya kutya ledger otaa kala ne operator metwokumwe. okudja meefila ponhele yokwiindila okwa pingenenwa keshilipaleko kutya oimaliwa otai kala po ngaashi ongeshefa tai twikile. ohatu fiki kongeshefa tai piti omayakulo okukala noimaliwa ya wana, tai henuka omalipilo okuhovela, tai dulu okutambula omalipilo ngeenge itai longo, tai kulu tai yooloka ko komufudo gwonhele

## etuvikilo

bitcoin deposits ota kendabala okugandjela oimaliwa ya endelela noya tenda ya kolelwa koshiivitho, nopena elineekelo, pondje yoshikwatelwa. oilonga yomukwatelwa gwonhele otai kulu pamwe neenamba do ledger nokuninga vali reserve. ouninginini woimaliwa otau kulu kanini pombada yomutwe netulilo mo ledger mongeshefa, tashi ningifa oimaliwa yomamiliyona okuningilwa mosekunte imwe meefo domabiliyona tai dulu okuninga

opena omalunduluko a yela:
- kape na okudja mo wa kutha mwene: ngeenge operator ova fa oimaliwa otai kala mongeshefa
- kape na ouholeko: epekithilo otali pula ounongononi
- okukala po akufimba: deposit otali kala po ashike ngaashi operator ta kala po. wallet nadi andjakanene oimaliwa yawo opo di kale po lela

ohatu teelela ounongo wa wallet u kale wa fa omufudo wa endelela, u na omalipilo a fa ongeshefa ya lightning network

## ledger

ledger oli li oshikwatelwa shomafetululo itashi dulu okulundululwa, shi na hash yoshifetululo sha djako noshi li sha nyolwa ko operator gwefo. omafo omafetululo a yoolokathana otaa longithwa meenghalo da yoolokathana. ledger ohaa liholola ye mwene, omafetululo gawo otaa monika komesho noka taa dulu okukanithwa, tashi pitike nande olye a tokole ngeenge oshi li pameta

ledger otaa ungaungwa ko operator umwe, ashike otaa kalekwa po pamwe kovaleta aveshe. nande operator ulipi ta dulu okuninga limwe, ndele ngeenge okwa kana nenge a ninga nai operator umwe ota ka tulwa, pamwe ne reserve. operator ta longo paife ota tseyithwa koshiivitho sha longithwa okunyola omafetululo gamwe gashikula

## deposit

deposit oli li oshifo sha tenda tashi dulu okutuma nokutambula oimaliwa, sha kolelwa ko miniscript. pokutulwa po, omapekaapeko omalipilo otaa tulwa po, na ngeenge okutambula oimaliwa otaku pula omukalo gwa nyolwa ke wallet. operator oku na okupitikilitha omalunduluko pokati ke deposit mefo limwe nosho wo okudja mo moshikwatelwa. ove na okupitikilitha deposit di fute omalipilo a lightning

omu li melitokolo la operator okuninga omalombwelo okumonifa oimaliwa nenge omalipilo a lightning omolwa deposit. ngee te shi ningi, eshi oshi na okunyolwa pamwe komukwatelwa gwa quorum, ne wallet oli na okukondjitha onyolo ei. omalombwelo nage haa li oshitukulwa she ledger, onghee wallet oli na okukondjitha eenyolo noku di kaleka po oumbangi

## omalipilo

omalunduluko pokati ke deposit, moshikwatelwa, namo lightning otaa futilwa operator gwe ledger. opena wo omalipilo haa tulwa koikwamaliwa akufimba nomukalo gwa tulwa po. aeshe otaa ningilwa etokolo pe deposit lipe tali tulwa po. omalipilo otaa dulu okulundululwa konima yomafiku a tulwa po, a pewa omafiku omapopilo noposhitukamunene shomundilo gwa tulwa po pokutulwa po. quorum otashi dulu okukala inashi nyola omafetululo taa eta omaupyakadi oo taa ka kutha ondjambi yawo

## omalunduluko

omukalo gwopanhu gwomalunduluko ogu li oilonga iivali pokati ke deposit mbali me ledger limwe: deposit otali indile okutuma oimaliwa. ngee opena oimaliwa ya wana, omukalo gwokukaleka oimaliwa nomukalo gwokulanda otau tulwa me ledger. ngee omukalo gwokulanda ogwa wanifwa manga ka gwa ya pefimbo, oimaliwa otai di komutumi okuya komutambuli kuhe na omalipilo ga operator. ngee efimbo la piti, omukalo otau kutwa, kuhe na omalipilo ga operator omanini. nomukalo gwa miniscript gwokulanda, eshi oshi li sha wana okupitikilitha nande deposit lilipi li gandje omayakulo gomayooloko ke deposit dikwao me ledger limwe

## lightning

operator ve na okanala ka lightning otava dulu okupitikilitha deposit di tume noku tambule mo lightning network. ngeenge deposit otali indile invoice ya lightning, operator ota li ningi mo lightning node yaye, ta indile ovakwatelwa va quorum va nyole pamwe opo ku shiivike kutya otava ka kwathelitha deposit ngeenge omalipilo a futu. wallet oli na okukaleka invoice eli la nyolwa pamwe oumbangi. ngeenge deposit otali indile efuto lyeinvoice ya lightning, operator ota futu mo lightning node yaye ta kutha oimaliwa me deposit konima yokumona preimage

ngeenge omufutu nomufutilwa aveshe ove li deposit me operator limwe, operator ota dulu okuundapitha meni nopokuhena okupititha mo lightning, ta kwathelitha nota kutha oimaliwa me deposit thiluthilu. eshi otashi henuka omalipilo okukutha omukalo nokukaleka eewanifilo doimaliwa da fa

## courier

omapulo omalunduluko ohaa lundulula ashike oimaliwa pokati ke deposit me ledger limwe. okulundulula oimaliwa pokati ko ledger, wallet ohadi longitha courier — omayakulo ge na deposit mo ledger mahapu taa eta omalunduluko pokati kawo. courier ota shiivitha ounene woimaliwa nomalipilo a ledger kokomesho ko relay. ngeenge wallet otali hala okutuma okuza me ledger A okuya me ledger B, otali ningi omukalo gwokukaleka oimaliwa me deposit lya courier ta indile kutya courier a ninge umwe okuza me deposit laye me ledger lokuya komufutilwa. shimha omikalo adishe da tulwa po wallet otali holola preimage komufutilwa, oo ta wanitha elunduluko lya courier. shimha sha holoka, courier ota longitha preimage oyo tuu okuwanitha elunduluko lyomutumi okuza ko courier

ou li omukalo gwopanhu gwa hash time-locked contract. ohatu teelela efimbo lya courier lyokuninga vali li kale pefimbo la yuka koshipala shefimbo lyokuya mo, tashi shi oukwatya kutya ngeenge wallet ka li holole nande, omikalo adishe otadi ka pita noka pe na oshipala tashi kanitha oimaliwa. kape na elineekelo la pumbiwa pondje yoshinima shefimbo sha ulikwa ko operator

courier ove na okulonga omalipilo a ledger: fee_in na fee_out a ledger aeshe hava yakula. wallet otali tokola ondjila yomalipilo ngaashi fee_out moshitopolwa na fee_in moshitudulwamo. courier otava dulu okulundulula omalipilo pandjugo yoshikwamaliwa, tava yukipaleke oimaliwa yawo nawa. wallet ohadi mono courier mo relay notadi hogolola shi etwa komalipilo, ounene, nenge omayakulo

## ekwatathano

ekwatathano alihe pokati ke wallet no operator, na pokati ko operator, otali longitha nostr relay. omafetululo a ledger otaa nyolwa ngaashi oiningwanima ya kalela opo yi kale po nawa, haa ningi omapekaapeko a dja omashiivifo a kalela. omapulo nomanyamukulo pokati ke wallet no operator ohaa kala ashike oiningwanima ya pokufupi ne na TTL ya pokufupi ko relay. operator ohava shiivitha oikwamaliwa yawo ngaashi oiningwanima ya dulu okulundululwa, tashi pitike wallet di kenge noku yelekanitha operator nopokuhena oshitopolwa shumwe shopokati

omusholondodo ou owa hala kutya wallet kadi pumbwa ekwatathano la kalela — otadi dulu okudja mo nefimbo lilile nokwaalukila kokunyola oiningwanima okuza ko relay shilipi tashi shi ningi. operator otava dulu okufikwa mo relay shilipi hava tale, nokuhogolola relay oshitokolo shomulongo, hashike shomukalo

## reserve na collateral

reserve ohadi kalekwa mo UTXO ne na oshimaliwa sha fa nenge sha pitilila oshikolitha shoimaliwa ye ledger, sha dulu okulandwa kovahapu va quorum, no operator ta dulu okukutha konima yefimbo lilile

collateral oshi li oimaliwa ya operator mwene, ya tulwa noya kalekwa po mo ledger dhovakwatelwa va quorum. omukwatelwa keshe oku na deposit ya collateral shi na oimaliwa sha tulwa ko operator sha kalekwa po pefimbo. oimaliwa ye ledger otai pingenena okuli haliyavali ya collateral sha nini shomukwatelwa ulipi, na quorum otashi pingenena pefimbo la pokufupi. eshi otashi shi oukwatya kutya omukutu wa collateral osha kala shi na oimaliwa ya wana okuhololela okukutha custody. deposit ya collateral shimwe otashi dulu okuyakula ledger mahapu opo shi ninge nawa noikwamaliwa, nande ngaha wallet nadi kale da hala operator ve na omafyuululwakalo a collateral a yoolokathana

oimaliwa otai pingenena pokuninga omalombwelo omape nenge omalipilo. operator ita dulu okuninga omalombwelo nenge omalipilo taa undulile oimaliwa ye ledger kombada ye reserve nenge kombada yehaliyavali ya collateral sha nini, shimwe shasha nini

## quorum

operator ohava indile operator vakwao va ye mo quorum shawo mokutula nokukalekapo collateral me ledger lyomukwatelwa. eindilo otali kwatela mo oshitokolo sha collateral (oshikolitha nefimbo) nomikalo dhomukwatelwa: omalipilo omanini a pumbwa deposit me ledger di wanithe. omukwatelwa keshe oku na okulonga ledger laye mwene notashi dulu okukutha collateral ya operator ngeenge operator okwa ulikwa a li ina wanifa po. ovakwatelwa ohava tulile omalipilo oposhitukamunene pokukala kwavo mo quorum — operator ita dulu okutula deposit nomalipilo kombeli yoshinima shomukwatelwa inashi hala, tashi amene ovakwatelwa okuza mokukutha oimaliwa inai etela mo sha konima yokulundulula custody

shimha quorum sha tulwa po, reserve otadi lundululwa mo UTXO ompe ya multisig. ovakwatelwa ohava nyola omafetululo a yuka nohava kwathelitha ngeenge operator okwa nyola oo inaa wanifa po. quorum oshinene otashi undulila ekwatathano ndele otashi shi oukwatya kutya operator oku na oshiponga shanini, okukala po okwa hapala, netokolo lyomakufiwo otali ningi elela nuupyakadi. wallet nadi kale da hala quorum oshinene

## okuthikamitha koikwamaliwa

omukalo ou owa pinga okudja mo wa kutha mwene nokuthikamitha koikwamaliwa. ovakwatelwa va quorum otava kala va halelwa thiluthilu okuninga sha li nai. pefimbo lyoilonga ya fa, ohava mono omalipilo omanini mo collateral, ndele ngeenge operator okwa ulikwa a li ina wanifa po otava dulu okukutha collateral ya operator ashishe me ledger lawo

ngeenge wallet otali dhiladhila kutya otali kondjithwa, otali dulu okutuminina omukwatelwa gwa quorum mokutuma kwa shiivikwa. omukwatelwa ota tula hash yomupulo me ledger laye pondjambi ya pokufupi, tashi ningi oumbangi wa tenda. ngeenge operator okwa fa okulonga omupulo, omukwatelwa oku na oumbangi nomauwa okutameka etokolo

ombudi yomalipilo a lightning otai landula omukalo wa fa gwokuthikamitha. operator oku shi shii ngeenge preimage okwa tambulwa, ndele wallet itali shi shii. nande ngaha omufutu ulipi ota dulu okugandjela wallet preimage. ombudi imwe ya shiivikwa otai tameke etokolo, okukuthwa kwe reserve, nokukuthwa kwa collateral. ondjambi yokuyiva omalipilo amwe oya pingenena, ndele oshiponga oshikulu, tashi ningi okuyiva kwa lightning inaku yuka koikwamaliwa nande tashi dulu okuhena ombangi nopokuhena ekwathelithilo lyomukwao

omhumbwe yokukala inai longa ko quorum ashishe shi pfumelelana. omukalo ou itau dulu okwaamena quorum tashi longo pamwe okuyiva, ndele omukutu wa collateral otashi shi oukwatya kutya etokolo lyomakufiwo otali futitha shi pitilile shimwe shi dja mo. ounongononi wongeshefa otau pitike wallet nomalandithilo okumona quorum sha fa sha puka manga inashi tulwa po oimaliwa

## efimbo

efimbo lya yela otali pekapekwa komufudo gwonhele. omayeleko itaa dulu okupitilila omafiku a shilika opo ku kalekwe po okukala tenda pefimbo lyomalunduluko

apa omayeleko manene a pumbiwa ohatu itavelele mokuyandja. ledger lyomapekaapeko lyokukriptika oli li oshikwatelwa sha merkle. omafetululo keshe otaa ulike kutya okwa ningwa konima yomafetululo aeshe a djako, ndele itaa yandja eeshilipaleko kombinga yoshinima pondje yoshikwatelwa. opo tu ninge okuyandja kwa andjuka, ohatu pula kutya eenyolo pamwe nadi kwatele mo hash yomafetululo gamwe ga djako okuza me ledger lyomunyoli. hash oyo otai tulwa moshifetululo osho, tai ningi oshitukulwa shoshikwatelwa nosho wo shomikwatelwa dhikwao adishe operator ha nyolele, tashi ningi oshikwatelwa shefimbo. eshi itashi dulu okuulika efimbo lya yela, ndele otashi dulu okuulika kutya omauyelele amwe okwa ningwa mokuyandja kwa tulwa po

## oumbangi wombudi

ohatu dulu okuulika omafo ombudi mokuhololela omauyelele a ningwa mokuyandja kwa puka. apa omauyelele inaa tulwa mo moilonga yongeshefa ya fa, otaa dulu okutwaalwa mo mokuninga oilonga ye na hash youmbangi. shimha sha tulwa moshifetululo sha nyolwa ko operator, oumbangi otau holoka wa ningwa ponhele ya puka mokuyandja:

- operator, a lombwela okukwathelitha deposit noimaliwa ya tumwa moshikwatelwa konhele ya tulwa po, ta nyola oshifetululo she ledger shi hena okwathelithilo sha yuka, ndele oshi na oshikwatelwa sha holola hash yomufiku sha pitilila omafiku a pitikwa manga inaku kwathelithwa

- operator, a ninga invoice ya lightning omolwa deposit, ta nyola oshifetululo she ledger inali kwathelitha deposit nonande preimage ya holoka moshikwatelwa

- enyolo pamwe ya lombwela hash ye ledger osho ya djako monyolo yayo ya shikula moshikwatelwa

- omukwatelwa gwa quorum she ledger la kondjithwa a kala ta longo ndele ina longa pamwe noumbangi wombudi momafiku a tulwa po

- okunyola nenge okunyola pamwe omafetululo a ledger inaa wanifa po

oumbangi wombudi ou na oumbangi noshikwatelwa shefimbo sha kwatitha hash ya tulwa po ke ledger lya operator a undilwa. oshikwatelwa oshi li omafetululo a nyolwa pamwe, keshe tali kwatela mo member_ledger_hash okuza me ledger la djako. ovapekapeki ohava landula oshikwatelwa nopokuhena okukonga, hava koleke kutya enyolo keshe oli li oshifetululo sha nyolwa, nokutya hash youmbangi otai fatana nomauyelele a tulwa mo

## okutambulula

shimha ledger la ninga itali monika nenge inali wanifa po, ovakwatelwa va quorum otava dulu okuninga okutwikila kwawo kwe ledger okuza poshifetululo sha shikula. ove na okulonga quorum oshipe nokugandja oumbangi wa collateral. ovakwatelwa ove na okuungaunga okulanda reserve da djako oshitopolwa shetokolo lyomikwatelwa da dja tadi shikula. omufindani gwetokolo eli ota tula oshifetululo shokukutha me ledger laye, navakwao otava tula oshifetululo shokuyandja. wallet otadi twikile nokulongitha ledger la fa, hadi tambula ashike omanyamukulo a nyolwa pamwe ko quorum. akufimba, ngeenge omanyamukulo kae na enyolo ya teelelwa, wallet oli na okupula ongeshefa nokunyola omafetululo a ledger opo li tseyithiwe omalunduluko mo custody

ngeenge okuhena okuwanifa po otaku monika sha fa oshiponga (pashiholelwa, ledger otali kala itali monika pomafiku a tulwa po) elunduluko lya custody oli na okukala nelitulemo: ashike oshikolitha she reserve sha pumbiwa okuhololela oimaliwa ye ledger otashi tumwa ketokolo, noshiyoolwamo otashi galulilwa koshiivitho sha operator. okuungaunga kwa collateral itaku kumithwa

ngeenge oumbangi wokuhena okuwanifa po ou li po, oshikolitha sha pitilila reserve sha pumbiwa otashi andjukwa sha fa kovakwatelwa va quorum, na collateral mo ledger dhovakwatelwa otashi pitikwa shi kuthwe

## oukolele wongeshefa

etokelo limwe la yukilila okuli okuninga oifitu ya operator hava longo pamwe. konima yokulonga oimaliwa ihapu mo ledger gawo, ohava longo pamwe okudja mo, hava yivi oimaliwa ya pitilila collateral sha kana. ongeshefa otai dulu okwiindjipaleka eshi, shimha ashike momahala oimaliwa yomeni ya pitilila collateral sha kwatitha kongeshefa inai kufiwa. omalipilo a collateral omanene na quorum oshinene sha andjuka otashi shi oukwatya kutya oupyakadi ou ihau holoka kanini, ndele ou na okuninga nonkatu ne wallet adishe itadi dulu okutokola ongeshefa aishe. ponhele yaasho, omalandithilo naa nyolemo omapekaapeko ga operator a etwa komapekapeko ongeshefa ngaashi omikalo dhokutokola

## exulilo

ohatu ulike ongeshefa ya collateral tai pula etokolo lyomakufiwo okuyiva, ndele etokolo lyomakufiwo otali undulile collateral sha li moshiponga meendelelo shi pitilile oimaliwa ya dja yokuyivwa. ohatu longitha ongeshefa ei okwaamena ledger omapekaapeko a amenwa ke reserve adishe. ledger aa otaa yakula oshifo omolwa wallet itadi longo mokupitikana nomalipilo a tulwa po. omafudo a ledger otaa yambidida omikalo dhokulanda dha miniscript dha wana okukutha omukalo gwopanhu. ongeshefa otai kulu ya fa tai landula omutwe, tashi pitike ongeshefa inene i gandjele wallet domabiliyona noimaliwa ya pitilila ongeshefa yomalipilo yonale
